What happens after you book

We take payment by bank transfer. There is no card payment on this website. Four steps, and you can see all of them before you give us anything.

  1. Pick your dates. You will see the total in naira with VAT already in it.
  2. Give us your name, email and phone number. You get a booking reference straight away.
  3. We show you our First Bank account and the reference to use as the narration. Transfer within four hours and the room stays yours.
  4. Send us the receipt on WhatsApp. We check it against the account and email your confirmation that evening.

If the four hours pass before the transfer lands, the hold simply lapses and those dates go back on sale. Nothing is charged and you can start again in one tap.

Arriving early or leaving late

Both are requests, not purchases. Tick one and we will answer it in the same email that confirms your booking, along with any fee.

Frequently asked questions

The questions people actually send us, answered plainly. If yours is not here, send us a message and we will add it.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our First Bank account. Once you book we show you the account details and a booking reference to use as the transfer narration, and we email both to you as well so you still have them after you close the tab. There is no card payment on this website.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

Send us your transfer receipt on WhatsApp. We check it against the account and email your confirmation the same evening. Somebody has to look at a bank statement, which is why it is that evening and not four minutes later.

Is my room held while I transfer?

Yes, for four hours. Transfer inside that window and the room is yours, pending our confirmation. If the four hours pass before the money lands, the hold simply lapses and those dates go back on sale. Nothing is charged and you can book again straight away.

Can I book more than one room?

Email rhfguesthouseltd@gmail.com and we will arrange it by hand. With eighteen beds we would rather check the diary ourselves than let software guess.

How long can I stay?

One night, or as many nights as you like. There is no maximum. People stay with us for weeks at a time and the kitchen, the laundry and the mini-mart are there for exactly that.

Can I book by the hour or for the day?

No. Bookings are by the night.

Can I check in early or check out late?

Both can be requested when you book. Early check-in from 1:00 PM and late check-out until 1:00 PM, both subject to whether the room is free on the day. A fee may apply and we will tell you what it is in the same email that confirms your booking, so you never agree to a number you have not seen. Is my car safe?

There is adequate parking inside the gate and security on site. The gate and the front desk are both staffed at every hour, so there is somebody awake at three in the morning if you arrive then, or if you just want to check on the car.

How do I get here from the airport?

About forty minutes by road. Come out of Nnamdi Azikiwe onto Airport Road heading towards town, then turn off for Apo and ask for Apo Resettlement, Zone A.
