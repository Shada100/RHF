How this works

We take payment by bank transfer. There is no card payment here. Four steps, and you can read all four before you give us anything.

1. Pick your dates and a room. You see the total in naira with VAT already in it, before anything else happens.
2. Give us your name, email and phone number. We give you a booking reference straight away.
3. We show you our account number and your reference. Transfer within four hours and the room stays yours.
4. Send the receipt on WhatsApp. We check it against the account and email your confirmation that evening.
