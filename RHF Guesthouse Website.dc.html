FAQS

Frequently asked questions.

What people ask us before they book, in the order they ask it.

Will there be light and water?

[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]

Generator hours, whether there is inverter backup, and whether water is borehole or tanker. This is the first thing an Abuja guest wants to know, so the question stays on the page with the hole in it until you give us the answer.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer. The account name is shown at the point of transfer. There is no card payment here. You pick your dates and a room, you get a reference, we show the account details, and you send the receipt to us on WhatsApp.

What time can I check in and out?

[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]

Standard check-in and check-out times, and whether early arrival or late departure carries a charge. The desk is staffed around the clock either way, so a late arrival is never a problem.

Can I cancel?

[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]

The notice period and whether a transfer is refundable, moveable or held as credit. Guests will look for this before they transfer money, so it is worth writing plainly.

Is my car safe?

There is adequate parking inside the gate and security on site. The gate and the front desk are staffed at every hour of the night, so arriving at three in the morning is not a problem.

Can I work here?

WiFi runs day and night, there is a study desk in every room, air conditioning throughout, and the street is quiet. The Executive and the Luxury also have a fridge.

Will I be fed?

Room service, a fully equipped kitchen you are welcome to use, and a mini-mart on site. Breakfast on the morning you arrive is included if you book the Luxury Room.

Is the house accessible?

[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]

Whether there is step-free access from the car park to reception, which rooms are on the ground floor, and whether any bathroom has a level-access shower.
